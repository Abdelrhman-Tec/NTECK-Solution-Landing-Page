EN headline: **Built for Founders at Every Stage.**
Cards:
- Startup founders: AR **بتأسس Startup وعايز تطلع أول نسخة من منتجك؟ بنساعدك تحدد الأولويات وتبني MVP قابل للتطوير.** EN **Turn your startup vision into a focused MVP built around your most important product goals.**

## Services
AR **خدمات تقنية تساعد فكرتك تكبر.** EN **Engineering Services for Every Product Stage.**
Six cards with icon, benefit, deliverables, and functional inquiry CTA: Product Discovery (goals, users, scope, roadmap), MVP Development, Flutter Mobile Apps, Custom Web Applications, Laravel Backend & APIs, Product Improvement & Support. Avoid fixed cost/timeline promises.

## Process
AR **من أول مكالمة لحد إطلاق منتجك.** EN **A Clear Process from Idea to Launch.**
Discover (goals/users), Plan (scope/milestones/architecture), Build (implementation/reviews/testing), Launch (deployment/handover as agreed). Timeline visually responsive and direction-aware. Do not imply every engagement includes all phases.

## Why Us
AR **مش مجرد كود... بنهتم بالمنتج من أساسه.** EN **More Than Code. Thoughtful Engineering.**
Focus on direct technical communication, defined deliverables, maintainable code, product thinking, transparent process, practical choices. No invented awards/team size/years.
