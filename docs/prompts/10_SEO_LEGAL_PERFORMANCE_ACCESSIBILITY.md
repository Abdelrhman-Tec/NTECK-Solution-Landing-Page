# 10 — SEO, legal pages, performance and accessibility
Read `01_GLOBAL_RULES.md` first.

## SEO
Locale-specific metadata, canonical URLs, `hreflang`, Open Graph/Twitter card, sitemap, robots, semantic heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text, valid structured data **only for verified facts**. AR title: **NTech Labs | تطوير تطبيقات ومشاريع MVP للشركات الناشئة**. EN title: **NTech Labs | MVP & Mobile App Development for Startups**. Search intent: app development, MVP, startup product development in Egypt and Saudi Arabia; natural copy, no stuffing. Avoid fabricated business addresses, reviews, ratings, and client counts.

## Legal
Create bilingual Privacy and Terms pages with clearly flagged business-specific placeholders for review, not fabricated guarantees. Explain actual form data collection, contact purpose, processor/retention details only as implemented/verified. Require owner review before launch.

## Performance
Optimize LCP/INP/CLS; Next Image, font subsets, avoid layout shift, Server Components by default, lazy below-fold content if beneficial, no heavy 3D/video, minimal client JS, optimized Motion and reduced-motion fallback.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA target: semantic controls, contrast, visible focus, skip link, correct AR/EN direction, screen-reader navigation, form errors, focus management, keyboard operation, reduced motion, responsive tap targets.

## Security
Server-side validation, safe URL handling, appropriate security headers/CSP (avoid breaking Google booking or email integration), no exposed secrets, dependency audit, secure external links. Do not assert an audit passed without running it.

## Acceptance
Localized metadata, legal routes, sitemap/robots, mobile performance review, accessibility checks and documented unresolved items.
